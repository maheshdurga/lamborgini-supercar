BORN OUT OF PURE DETERMINATION

Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, Automobili Lamborghini established its headquarters in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy. What started as a challenge to automotive tradition became an icon of futuristic design and atmospheric V12 sound.

Every vehicle crafted at Sant'Agata is a synthesis of cutting-edge technology, hand-stitched Italian upholstery, and aerodynamic sculpture.
